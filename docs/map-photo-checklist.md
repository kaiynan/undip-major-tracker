# UNDIP Tembalang Campus — Field Photo Checklist

This checklist is provided for the campus owner/survey team to collect ground-truth photographic reference of buildings and landmarks across Universitas Diponegoro (UNDIP) Tembalang campus.

Photos collected will be stored in `map-data/photos/` and used as reference to refine the 16-bit pixel-art rendering.

---

## 1. Photography Guidelines
- **Lighting:** Shoot during clear daytime or late afternoon (golden-hour, 15:30–17:00 WIB) for clear shadow delineation.
- **Angles per Building:**
  1. **Front View (`<id>_front.jpg`):** Eye-level or slightly elevated view showing the main entrance and facade.
  2. **Three-Quarter / Isometric Angle (`<id>_45deg.jpg`):** 45-degree angle showing roofline, depth, and side elevation.
  3. **Side View (`<id>_side.jpg`):** Profile view highlighting wing depth, skybridges, or auxiliary structures.
- **File Naming Pattern:** `<id>_<angle>.jpg` (e.g., `ft_front.jpg`, `ft_45deg.jpg`, `widya-puraya_front.jpg`).
